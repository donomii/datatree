Datatree

Browse the directory opened by Datatree, explore a sample, or open a ZIP or snapshot in browser memory.

Focus the scene and use WASD to move, arrows to look, and Enter to select. On a gamepad, use the sticks to move and look and the bottom face button to select.

In VR, use the left stick to move and the right stick to turn. Aim at a plaque and press the trigger or pinch with a tracked hand.

Choose a branch to open it or a leaf to read its value.
